Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

A profession skill is shown as a zoomed-in, pixelated icon. Each wrong guess zooms out and sharpens it. Hints unlock after 3 guesses (profession) and 6 guesses (slot). Unlimited guesses.

When does the puzzle change?

Every day at midnight, Paris time (00:00, daylight saving included). Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same day.

How many guesses do I get?

As many as you like: you can't lose. Your score is your number of guesses.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it's free and needs no sign-up. Your games, statistics and streak are kept in your browser only.

Is this an official Guild Wars 2 game?

No. GW2dle is an unofficial fansite, not affiliated with or endorsed by ArenaNet or NCSOFT. Names and icons come from the official Guild Wars 2 API.
